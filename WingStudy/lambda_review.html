Objective: increase thrust while penalizing root torque

ΔS = (Tnew − Tcurrent) / Tref − λ (Mnew − Mcurrent) / Mref

λ is the dimensionless torque weight. Increasing λ makes torque reduction more valuable relative to a normalized thrust gain. λ = 0 favors thrust alone. For these fixed references, λ = 0.75, 1.25 and 1.75 penalize torque progressively more strongly. A change is accepted when ΔS exceeds the saved positive tolerance; λ is neither a torque limit nor a physical efficiency.

Methods in brief

Each chord starts with positive and negative width trials. The better positive score change wins. Subsequent root-to-tip passes use that direction; a failed full step gets one half-step trial, then that chord retires. Outboard zero chords remove a contiguous suffix, so the actual span can shrink. These are bounded local searches; completion is not proof of a global optimum.

Torque bands are used after the search. The representative maximizes mean single-wing thrust within the band. The full frontier and per-lambda envelopes remain visible separately, so band selection does not hide tradeoffs or imply that every representative is Pareto optimal.

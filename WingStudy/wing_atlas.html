Selected conditions and interpretation
Fullness is area / (span × root chord). Aspect ratio uses one wing. Colors describe geometry; thrust / torque is dimensional, not energy efficiency. Density reflects sampling effort, not probability or performance quality. Only a matched conditions group supports a controlled comparison of shape effects. Even then, shapes may differ in size.

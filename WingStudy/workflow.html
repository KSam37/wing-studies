1. Set the constraint

Limit on the final settled cycle’s peak absolute roll moment at one wing root. This is your root-level limit, after your own drivetrain calculation.

Maximize mean forward thrust among rigid wings that stay within this limit. Thrust/moment ratio is not the ranking objective.

2. Sweep chord geometry

Equally spaced root-to-tip nodes. Leading-edge sweep and incidence are zero.

Smooth local exploration

Smooth interpolation preserves the source span and taper. Neighboring chord variations are correlated to avoid a jagged outline. Edit individual min/max bounds below. The baseline is included; this is a local sampled search, not a guarantee of a global optimum. More nodes require more span panels, which are increased automatically. 3. Hold motion fixed

Default range: −26° to +34° (4° center, 30° half-stroke). One isolated wing is simulated; estimated pair thrust is twice its thrust. Ptera requires positive forward speed.

Water and numerical settings

Fresh water at approximately 20 °C. Prescribed/shortened wakes are screening approximations; check mesh, time step and wake convergence on finalists.

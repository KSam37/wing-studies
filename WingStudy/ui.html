Objective and reference scale

Rank the physical right wing by final-cycle mean forward thrust divided by sampled peak absolute roll moment about its root hinge. Exactly one wing is simulated, with its root at the airplane moment reference. Native roll plots show that wing’s moment. Estimated pair thrust is twice its thrust and excludes wing interaction. Native wind-axis roll and body-axis hinge roll use opposite signs, with equal magnitudes. Zero or negative mean thrust and numerical-zero moments do not produce valid scores. This is an aerodynamic shape metric, not motor efficiency.

The ratio T/M has units 1/m. A smaller geometrically similar wing can have a larger dimensional ratio. We keep the reference grid span fixed; trailing zero chords can shorten the active wing within a sweep and also report the dimensionless score L·T/M, where L is exposed half-span. Similarity scaling T∝s² and M∝s³ assumes comparable velocity fields and force coefficients. Changing span at fixed frequency changes flapping speed and reduced frequency; water viscosity changes Reynolds number. Scaling alone is not a validated replacement for a new solve.

Chord-node search

Chord nodes are equally spaced from root to tip. Each node has an inclusive linspace range with its own sample count. The optional non-increasing constraint allows equal adjacent lengths and prunes increasing branches before case generation. Leading-edge sweep and incidence default to zero and stay constant. Static tails are disabled for isolated-wing studies. Motion, water and numerical settings remain fixed. Zero chords are allowed only as an outboard suffix; at least two positive sections are required. Zeros omit sections, so the wing ends at the last positive section. Identical active section geometry is simulated once per study, with entered designs retained as aliases.

This is an exhaustive search over the admissible sampled grid, not a continuous optimizer. Use a small coarse grid first, then refine promising regions. The case budget limits admitted cases. Each chord interval needs at least one span panel; check mesh, delta time and wake convergence for finalists.

Timing, outputs and confidence

The 3D wing-shape landscape uses wing area, aspect ratio and single-wing thrust/roll moment. Each measured case is a neutral top-view wing thumbnail, normalized in size. At fixed span A·AR = span², so interpolation follows the admissible curve instead of fabricating a sheet. Varying-span data uses a piecewise-linear triangulated surface inside the sampled convex hull. All measured shapes remain visible; duplicate area/AR coordinates use the best sampled ratio for the interpolated upper envelope. Interpolated geometry is an estimate, not another simulation.

The preview ETA uses similar local runs where available. During a run it updates from recent completed cases and includes native plot/animation saving. Treat it as approximate; machine load, wake model and mesh affect runtime.

Per case: four native Ptera PNG time plots, animation, and separate left/right full-scale SVG outlines. Shared raw CSV includes physical single-wing forces and hinge roll moments. Comparisons use PNG and CSV, with top performers and the winning geometry. Historical studies are retained but require rerunning for the new objective; old net roll or motor metrics are not substituted.

The solver remains stock rigid prescribed-motion UVLM in room-temperature water. Forward speed must remain positive. Custom flapping waveforms and manually constructed wing shapes remain available. Stiffness, membrane deformation, separated flow, body drag, drivetrain behavior and free flight are not modeled. Predictions need convergence and physical validation.

Set up a new study

Choose wings, plate models, frequencies and numerics. The page checks the spec with the same rules as the study tools. Submit it on GitHub, where it becomes a pull request, or download it. Studies are solved on a contributor's own computer, and their results appear in the atlas once the results pull request is merged.

About the study

Kind of study

Each kind runs an existing solver path unchanged.

Structure, plate models and frequencies

Plain-weave carbon sheets; "+ rod" adds the 5/3 mm carbon tube along the leading edge (the hardware).

Wings

Atlas designs you picked (use "Add to a new study" on any design in the atlas), plus outlines you enter. Chords are 8 equally spaced stations from root to tip; zeros may only end the wing.

Scrambled-Sobol outlines with the campaign rules: 200 mm root, each station 30–100% of the previous one, chords under 10 mm end the wing.

Numerics

Every case is solved at each setting. The campaigns used 8 × 4 panels, 50 steps per cycle, 4 cycles; every elastic solve is also checked at dt/2 and dt/4.

Spec

Date and a short name; it becomes the folder name.

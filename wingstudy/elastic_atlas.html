Analysis type is the physics a wing was simulated with. Rigid wings have no material, so construction and thickness apply to the elastic analyses only. Sheets are carbon fibre; the rod is a 5/3 mm carbon tube along the leading edge. The boundary condition says how the model holds the sheet at the leading edge (hover a chip for the details); it follows from analysis type and construction, and in every model the root is fixed to the flapping hub. λ is the torque penalty in the search score S = T/Tref − λ·M/Mref: a larger λ steers the search toward lower root torque. Designs found any other way (the torque-budget search, shape maps, sweeps, reruns) have no λ.

How elasticity modelling changes the results

The same wings solved with progressively more structure. Chordwise elasticity lets the sheet twist passively about the leading edge and is compared against the same outline as a rigid plate. Spanwise + chordwise elasticity also lets the sheet bend along the span and is compared against the same wing with chordwise elasticity only. Every comparison holds the outline, motion and numerics fixed, so the difference is the structural model alone.
